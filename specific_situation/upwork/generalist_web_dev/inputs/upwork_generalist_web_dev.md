## Summary

We're an international web development agency with a team of five developers, and we're adding a
sixth. This is a permanent full-time position, not a one-off project.

Pay is $1,000 to $1,500 per month depending on experience, paid monthly through Upwork. Around 40
hours a week, Monday to Friday, with occasional weekend work when something urgent lands. Reviewed
after three months.

What you'll work on: new builds and landing pages, redesigns and rebuilds, speed and conversion
optimization, migrations between platforms, integrations and automations, plus ongoing fixes and
updates for existing clients.

Platforms: WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace and GoHighLevel. You need real hands-on experience
with at least two or three and the ability to pick up the rest fast. Only knowing one isn't enough
here.

## How we work with AI

AI is how most of the work gets done. Our primary tool is Claude and Claude Code, everything else is
secondary. You use it for writing code, building layouts, debugging, adapting content and research,
and you prompt precisely enough to get usable output fast rather than after ten attempts.

And you supervise it rather than just prompting it. You read what it produces, catch what's wrong,
and fix it by hand when it goes off track. That means understanding HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Liquid
and PHP well enough to take over. This is the part most applicants get wrong, so be specific about
how you do it.

## What we need

Availability during European business hours, reachable and responsive throughout the day.

Speed with precision. You test your own work before it leaves your hands.

Several projects in parallel, realistic estimates, deadlines hit, problems flagged early rather than
at the last minute.

Fluent English. You'll speak directly with clients, give clear updates, and explain technical things
in plain language.

Comfort with multilingual sites. A lot of our work is in German, Spanish and French. You use AI for
content in languages you don't speak and check carefully that nothing ends up broken or wrong.

Nice to have: page builders (Elementor, Divi, WPBakery), Shopify theme customization, GoHighLevel
funnels and CRM setup, Figma, Zapier or Make, SEO and performance work, and German, Spanish or
French.

## How this works

One paid test project in two stages. We send a brief, you come back with two design directions for
the page, around 48 hours. We pick one and give feedback, you build it out, another 48 hours. Both
stages paid as fixed-price milestones.

If that goes well, two weeks of real client work at a reduced rate, then the full-time position.

## To apply

Start your proposal with the word GENERALIST, then answer the screening questions. Applications that
skip this won't be reviewed.

## Screening questions

1. Which of the five platforms (Shopify, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, GoHighLevel) have you worked
   with, and roughly how many projects on each?
2. Share links to 3 to 5 live sites you built or significantly worked on, and note which platform
   each one uses.
3. How do you use AI in your workflow: which tools, for what, and have you used Claude or Claude
   Code? Give one example of something the AI got wrong that you caught and fixed.
4. How many hours per week can you commit, what's your time zone, and can you work during European
   business hours?
5. We start with one paid test project: two design directions first, then you build the one we pick.
   Are you open to that, and what's your hourly rate for ongoing work?
