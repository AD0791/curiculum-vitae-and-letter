## What CMMB Asks For

The posting's requirement is one line: *"CV, Cover Letter, and copies of academic diplomas and
certificates."* Three items, no single-PDF rule — so they go as **three separate attachments**,
in English, which is the language the job description itself is written in.

### Attachment Checklist

1.  `[ ]` `outputs/Alexandro_Disla_Resume_CMMB_EN.pdf` — Curriculum Vitae
2.  `[ ]` `outputs/Alexandro_Disla_CoverLetter_CMMB_EN.pdf` — Cover Letter
3.  `[ ]` `outputs/Alexandro_Disla_Diplomas_Certificates.pdf` — CTPEA diploma, secondary
    diploma, and any certificates

Item 3 is **not in this repository**. Drop the scans into `credentials/` using the naming
scheme in `credentials/README.md`, then run `./build_cmmb.sh` — the script converts images to
PDF and merges them in order. It refuses to produce the file and warns you if the directory is
empty.

> **Before sending:** open the merged PDF and confirm the CTPEA diploma page is present and
> legible. The posting names diplomas as a required part of the dossier.

### The French pair — built, held in reserve

`Alexandro_Disla_CV_CMMB_FR.pdf` and `Alexandro_Disla_CoverLetter_CMMB_FR.pdf` are produced by
the same build. **Do not attach them to the first email.** The posting body is in English and
five attachments clutter a screener's inbox. Send them if CMMB replies in French, asks for a
French dossier, or if the interview is scheduled in French — at which point they are ready and
cost nothing.

## Points to Have Ready for the Interview

*   **The DATIM/MER line is the strongest thing in the application.** Be ready to say which MER
    indicators were reported, on what periodicity, and who validated them before submission. If
    the letters need to name specific indicator codes, add them only after confirming which ones
    were actually handled.
*   **Referee to offer first**: Davidson Adrien, former Director M&E, Caris Foundation,
    (+509) 4460-6638 — the health-sector referee CMMB is most likely to recognise.
*   **"DSS"** appears unexpanded in the posting's introduction. Neither letter uses it. If it
    comes up, ask what it stands for rather than guessing — most plausibly the departmental
    health directorates.
*   **Certification in M&E or statistics** is listed as "a plus" and is the one qualification
    not currently held. If asked, the honest answer is the CTPEA applied-economics degree —
    a four-year economics and statistics cycle — plus five years of applied practice.
