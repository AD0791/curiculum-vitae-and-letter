## I. Organizational Overview

Samaritan's Purse is a nondenominational evangelical Christian organization providing spiritual
and physical aid worldwide. Since 1970, Samaritan's Purse has supported people affected by war,
poverty, natural disasters, disease, and famine while sharing God's love through Jesus Christ.
Samaritan's Purse Haiti began operations following the 2010 earthquake and continues to implement
humanitarian, development, ministry, health, WASH, livelihoods, protection, and education
programs.

## II. Position Summary

The Senior Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) Officer provides
country-level leadership and oversight of MEAL systems across all Samaritan's Purse Haiti
programs. The position ensures that programs generate credible evidence, monitor approved
results, remain accountable to affected populations, and use learning to improve program quality
and decision-making. The Senior MEAL Officer advises leadership and Program Managers and promotes
consistent, ethical, and compliant MEAL practices throughout the Country Office.

## IV. Key Responsibilities

### 1. MEAL Strategy and Quality Assurance

- Lead the design, implementation, and periodic review of the Country Office MEAL framework.
- Establish and maintain standards for results frameworks, indicators, monitoring plans,
  evidence, accountability, learning, data protection, and document retention.
- Ensure MEAL systems comply with approved proposals, organizational policies, donor
  requirements, and recognized humanitarian standards.
- Conduct data-quality assessments and field-verification visits, document findings, and monitor
  corrective actions through completion.
- Advise the Deputy Country Director and leadership team on program quality, performance risks,
  evidence, and learning.
- Maintain preparedness to rapidly establish monitoring, assessment, accountability, and
  data-management systems during emergency responses, including rapid needs assessments,
  post-distribution monitoring, and outcome monitoring.

### 2. Program Design and Proposal Development

- Support needs assessments, contextual analysis, stakeholder mapping to inform program design.
- Work with program development personnel, technical teams, and Program Managers to develop
  theories of change, logical frameworks, indicators, targets, monitoring plans, accountability
  mechanisms, evaluation activities, and MEAL budgets.
- Ensure appropriate MEAL plans and tools are incorporated into funding proposals.
- Support proposals with credible needs data, program results, beneficiary information, and
  lessons learned.
- Review MEAL budgets, staffing, tools, and resources to ensure adequate capacity.

### 3. Monitoring, Data Management and Protection

- Oversee routine monitoring and ensure that program information is collected accurately,
  efficiently, ethically, and on time.
- Design and standardize quantitative and qualitative data-collection tools and digital
  workflows.
- Ensure consistent, secure, and appropriately controlled management of program and beneficiary
  data.
- Enforce informed-consent, confidentiality, access-control, storage, transfer, retention, and
  data-protection standards.
- Safeguard personally identifiable, sensitive, safeguarding-related, and complaint information.

### 4. Assessments, Evaluations and Learning

- Lead or coordinate needs assessments, baselines, endlines, post-distribution monitoring,
  outcome monitoring, evaluations, and field research.
- Design appropriate quantitative and qualitative assessment and evaluation methodologies.
- Facilitate structured program reviews and learning processes.
- Document findings, good practices, corrective actions, and lessons learned, and monitor their
  application across programs.

### 5. Accountability to Affected Populations

- Strengthen safe and accessible mechanisms through which participants and communities can
  provide feedback, raise concerns, receive responses, and influence program improvement.
- Ensure feedback and complaints are handled confidentially, analyzed, referred appropriately,
  and tracked through closure.
- Analyze accountability information and recommend improvements to program design and
  implementation.
- Escalate significant accountability, safeguarding, or compliance concerns through appropriate
  management channels.

### 6. Reporting and Decision Support

- Support the preparation and quality review of routine, management, and donor reports.
- Ensure reported results are accurate, evidence-based, internally consistent, and submitted on
  time.
- Analyze data and communicate trends, gaps, risks, unintended results, and actionable
  recommendations to leadership and Program Managers.
- Prepare analytical products and dashboards for authorized audiences.
- Provide timely early warning when evidence indicates underperformance, data-integrity concerns,
  accountability or safeguarding risks, or material changes in program assumptions.

### 7. Leadership, Supervision and Capacity Development

- Establish clear priorities, review deliverables, support performance development, and promote
  consistent MEAL practices across programs and field locations.
- Assess staff capacity and provide training on monitoring, data quality, analysis,
  accountability, reporting, and learning.
- Promote the constructive use of evidence for program adaptation, stewardship, accountability,
  and continuous improvement.

### 8. Coordination, Representation and Spiritual Integration

- Serve as the principal liaison between the Samaritan's Purse IHQ MEAL function and the Haiti
  Country Office.
- Collaborate with Program Managers, technical advisors, Finance, Operations, HR, Communications,
  Security, and leadership to support integrated program management.
- Represent Samaritan's Purse, when delegated, in relevant clusters, technical working groups,
  government meetings, and interagency forums.
- Assist in leading weekly staff devotions.
- Model Christ-centered leadership, integrity, humility, and servant-hearted engagement.
- Perform additional responsibilities as assigned by the Deputy Country Director.

## V. Supervisory Responsibilities

- Directly supervise assigned MEAL Officers, Assistants, Enumerators, and temporary
  data-collection personnel.
- Provide technical oversight and quality assurance to personnel with monitoring, evidence,
  accountability, data-management, or reporting responsibilities.

## VI. Core Competencies and Technical Requirements

- Strong technical leadership, supervision, coaching, and capacity-development skills.
- Strong quantitative and qualitative research, monitoring, evaluation, and data-analysis
  capabilities.
- Ability to design results frameworks, indicators, monitoring plans, assessments, evaluations,
  databases, and data-quality processes.
- Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and proficiency with KoboToolbox or ODK.
- Proficiency with at least one statistical, database, or visualization platform, such as SPSS,
  Stata, R, Power BI, Microsoft Access, or an equivalent platform.
- Knowledge of project-cycle management, results-based management, adaptive management, and
  recognized MEAL practices.
- Working knowledge of humanitarian principles and relevant standards, including Sphere, the Core
  Humanitarian Standard, IASC guidance, and applicable sector standards.
- Excellent written, verbal, analytical, facilitation, and presentation skills.
- Ability to communicate technical findings clearly to nontechnical audiences and advise
  leadership and Program Managers effectively.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities and remain calm and professional under pressure.
- Fluency in English, French, and Haitian Creole required.

## VII. Education and Experience

- Bachelor's degree in monitoring and evaluation, statistics, economics, international
  development, public health, social sciences, information management, humanitarian studies, or a
  related field required; master's degree preferred.
- Minimum of five years of progressively responsible experience in MEAL, research, or program
  quality, including at least three years with a humanitarian or international development
  organization.
- Demonstrated experience leading, supervising, or providing technical oversight to MEAL
  personnel or multidisciplinary teams.
- Experience supporting program design, proposal development, and donor reporting preferred.

## VIII. Physical Requirements and Working Environment

- Office-based position requiring regular computer use, travel within Haiti, and participation in
  field visits and assessments.
- Forty-eight-hour workweek with additional hours or temporary deployment as required during
  emergencies or critical reporting periods.
- Exposure to dynamic security conditions, public-health risks, and operationally challenging
  environments.
- Ability to work in variable field conditions, including hot, humid, remote, or
  infrastructure-limited environments.
