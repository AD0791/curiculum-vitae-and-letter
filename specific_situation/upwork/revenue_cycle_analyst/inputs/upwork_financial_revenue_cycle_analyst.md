## Summary

Financial & Revenue Cycle Analyst  for Medical Billing Role

Remote

We are a growing U.S. psychiatric practice seeking a highly analytical professional to join our
financial and revenue-cycle operations team.

Prior medical billing experience is not required. We will train you from the ground up.

We are specifically interested in candidates with strong backgrounds in finance, mathematics,
actuarial science, economics, statistics, accounting, engineering, or another quantitatively
rigorous field.

This is not intended to be a traditional data-entry medical billing position. We need someone who
can understand complicated systems, investigate discrepancies, work accurately with financial
information, and determine why something happened rather than simply follow a checklist.

Quantitative Analyst

Strong Microsoft Excel skills are required.

Experience with SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, or similar analytical tools is valuable but not

## What we will teach you

You do not need previous experience in medical billing.

We will train you in:

U.S. healthcare reimbursement
Health insurance
Medical claims
CPT and diagnosis coding fundamentals
Insurance payer rules
Payment posting and reconciliation
Accounts receivable
Claim denials
Appeals
Underpayment investigation
Insurance portals
Revenue-cycle management
Healthcare financial operations

You will learn how money moves through a U.S. medical practice from the time a patient is seen
until the practice receives and reconciles payment.

## What the work involves

A significant part of your initial role will involve medical billing and revenue-cycle operations.

However, we are specifically hiring someone with a strong financial and analytical background
because we want this person to contribute beyond routine billing.

Examples include:

Investigating why claims were denied or underpaid
Reconciling claims, payments, contractual adjustments, and patient balances
Identifying systematic payer problems
Analyzing reimbursement by payer and service
Measuring actual collection rates
Monitoring accounts receivable
Finding financial discrepancies
Building financial reports and dashboards
Improving revenue-cycle processes

## The person we want

You should be someone who:

Has unusually strong analytical reasoning
Is very comfortable with numbers
Notices inconsistencies
Wants to understand why numbers do not reconcile
Can learn complex systems quickly
Is highly accurate and detail-oriented
Can work independently on unfamiliar problems
Communicates clearly in written English

We value reasoning ability and ability to learn more than prior healthcare experience.
