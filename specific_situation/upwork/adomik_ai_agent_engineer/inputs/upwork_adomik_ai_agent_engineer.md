We are looking for a Freelance AI Agent Engineer to join Adomik's Tech team on a long-term,
full-time engagement.

You will help build the AI agents and MCP integrations that allow our clients to query their
advertising performance and monetization data in natural language.

This is a hands-on production role focused on Python, backend engineering, data and applied AI. You
will work closely with our Tech team, Product, Data Engineers and Data Scientists.

You will be expected to take features from design to production. At Adomik, you ship features, not
tickets.

## ABOUT US

Adomik is the agentic AI platform for advertising performance and monetization. We unify fragmented
advertising data and power specialized AI agents, including JP (Jean-Pierre), our AI companion, that
work alongside teams to understand performance, make decisions, and act faster across every
workflow.

Our platform combines a powerful Data Engine, connecting and normalizing data from ad servers, SSPs,
exchanges, and demand platforms, with purpose-built AI agents designed for real advertising
workflows: revenue analysis, deal optimization, yield management, forecasting, and more.

Over 100 leading media and advertising companies across 20 countries rely on Adomik daily, including
The Washington Post, Microsoft, Tripadvisor, Hearst, BBC, and Amazon.

## WHAT YOU WILL DO

- Build and evolve AI agents and MCP tools on top of Adomik's advertising data platform.
- Develop Python backend services and REST APIs.
- Connect data processing, analytical algorithms and applied statistics to production features.
- Work on use cases such as revenue analysis, anomaly detection and explanations of revenue
  variations.
- Design reliable pipelines where numerical computations are deterministic and language models
  handle language-related tasks.
- Work with Snowflake and AWS services, including AWS Bedrock.
- Deliver features end to end, from data stored in Snowflake to production-ready agents and product
  features.
- Improve performance and reliability through asynchronous programming, caching and sound
  engineering practices.
- Contribute to testing, code reviews, CI/CD, automation and technical documentation.
- Collaborate closely with Tech, Product, Data Engineering and Data Science teams.

## REQUIRED EXPERIENCE

- At least 3 years of professional experience with Python.
- Professional experience with Ruby. Experience with Ruby on Rails is strongly preferred.
- Professional experience in data science or data analysis, including analytical methods and
  statistical reasoning.
- Strong SQL skills and experience writing complex analytical queries.
- Experience building backend applications or REST APIs.
- Experience with data processing, analytics or statistical methods.
- Experience with Git, testing, code reviews and modern software development practices.
- Ability to work independently and deliver production-ready features.
- Strong communication skills and a pragmatic approach to problem solving.
- Technical English.
- Full-time availability during the team's working hours described below.

Experience with MCP and LLM agents is a plus, but not mandatory. Candidates with strong Python, Ruby
and data experience who are motivated to learn MCP are welcome.

French is preferred but not required.
