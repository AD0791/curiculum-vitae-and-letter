## The One-PDF Rule

Samaritan's Purse requires **all documents in a single PDF**: *"Submit all documents as one PDF"*
and *"Incomplete applications will not be considered."* So the checklist below is the **contents
of the merged PDF**, not a list of separate attachments.

Attach exactly one file: `outputs/Alexandro_Disla_SeniorMEALOfficer_Application.pdf`

1.  `[ ]` **Cover Letter (English)** — page 1

scheme in `credentials/README.md`, then run `./build_sp.sh` — the script converts images to PDF
and merges everything in order.

> **Before sending:** open the merged PDF and confirm the NUI and NIF pages are actually there.
> The dossier is rejected as incomplete without them.
