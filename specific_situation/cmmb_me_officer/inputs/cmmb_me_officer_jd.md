## Introduction

The Monitoring and Evaluation Officer (M&E Officer) will work under the M&E director
supervision and will be responsible for supporting strategic information, monitoring and
evaluation, health management information systems and performance reporting for all HSD/Santé
components (RMCNH, WASH, RBF, Disaster and Preparedness... She/he is responsible for ensuring
that data collected is comprehensive, clean, and valid. Additionally, the (M&E Officer) will
plan for and lead all SI related training activities at the health facilities/sites and DSS.
The (M&E Officer) will work to ensure the project deliverables are being addressed and will
work collaboratively. She/he will represent the project to host country institutions, agencies,
and stakeholders, sharing information and updates.

## Fonctions

### M&E related activities

- Coordinate all monitoring and evaluation for designated projects.
- Supervise and coordinate the collection of accurate monitoring reports from the HF (monthly,
  quarterly, semiannual, and annually) on all required indicators.
- Provide routine feedback on key outcome indicators to health facilities and senior management
  team.
- Coordinate the technical assistance to HF with the statisticians in the analysis of data to
  address gaps in program and services.
- Gather feedback from site visits to each HF to assess technical assistance needs, flow of data
  and provide appropriate capacity building trainings.

### HMIS related activities

- Support information technology infrastructure and management for data management tools
- Support the deployment of latest version at all sites.
- Reporting of problems or "bug" in system.
- Responsible for documenting all communication of technical issues that result in HF not having
  access to their informatics system which will thus result in a backlog and clinical staff not
  being able to access information and report.
- Ensure the continued deployment, enhancement and functioning of the database.
- Build the data culture at health facilities to improve data demand and data use and increase
  HF demand for quality information, methods, and tools, and facilitation of information use in
  decision making.
- Manage and provide training for data analysis to the Health Facilities.
- Provide technical assistance to promote monitoring and evaluation strategies to facilitate
  data usage and ownership in a manner that enhances the program and promotes sustainability
- valuable information for decision-making

## Qualifications Requises

- Degree in statistics, sociology, computer science, economics, demography, international
  development, or a related field from a recognized university. Certification in M&E or
  statistics a plus.
- Proven success in the design, implementation and operation of project monitoring and
  evaluation systems from project start to completion.
- Knowledge and experience in collecting and analyzing qualitative and quantitative data.
- Experience in planning and managing surveys.
- Experience in the development and improvement of data collection tools, including the use of
  applications (Open Data Tools kit) for mobile data collection.
- Experience in data quality assessment and monitoring.
- Experience in management and continuous training of field staff in monitoring and evaluation.
- Computer literacy in Microsoft packages (MS Word, MS Excel, MS Access, MS PowerPoint) and
  GSuite, and other relevant software is an asset.
- Excellent writing and data analytical skills
- Fluency in both written and spoken, French and Haitian Creole, English is a must

## Reading notes for this application

**The posting is two jobs in one.** The M&E half is classic routine monitoring — indicator
reporting cycles from health facilities, outcome-indicator feedback, DQA, surveys, ODK, field
training. The HMIS half is essentially systems support — IT infrastructure for data-management
tools, version deployment across sites, bug reporting, documenting outages that leave facilities
unable to report, keeping the database running. Very few M&E candidates can answer the second
half. Alexandro can, and the application is built to say so.

**Acronyms as used here.** *HF* = health facility. *HSD* = USAID/Haiti's Health Service Delivery
programming, the frame CMMB's Projet Santé work sat in. *SI* = strategic information. *RMCNH* =
reproductive, maternal, newborn and child health (RMNCH; *SRMNIA* in Haitian French usage).
*RBF* = results-based financing, *financement basé sur les résultats*, adopted nationally in
Haiti's health sector. *DSS* is not expanded on the posting — most plausibly the departmental
health directorates. Because it is ambiguous, neither cover letter uses the abbreviation; both
say *departmental health directorates* / *directions sanitaires départementales* instead.

**"Open Data Tools kit"** in the qualifications list is ODK — Open Data Kit.

**The database behind the HMIS bullets.** Haiti's national health information system is SISNU,
built on DHIS2, launched by the MSPP in 2013 with USAID support and now covering all ten
departments and more than 800 facilities. "Support the deployment of latest version at all
sites" and "ensure the continued deployment, enhancement and functioning of the database" almost
certainly refer to it or to a project instance alongside it.
