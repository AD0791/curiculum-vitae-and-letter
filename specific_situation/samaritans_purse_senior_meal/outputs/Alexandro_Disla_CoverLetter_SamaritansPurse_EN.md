de-duplication of beneficiary lists. Confidentiality, access control, and retention are decisions
made in the schema and the permissions, and I am comfortable making them and documenting them.

On reporting and decision support, I built and maintained program dashboards in Power BI, Quarto,
and mWater for weekly indicator monitoring, and automated activity reporting with Python and SQL
at Caris Foundation, reducing manual processing time by 40%. As an Applied Economist by training,

I have supervised field data-collection staff across multi-site programs, provided technical

through to closure — so that what communities tell us becomes evidence that changes the program
rather than correspondence that ends in a folder.

I am drawn to this role because Samaritan's Purse has served Haitian communities continuously
